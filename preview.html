What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.
OctOpus does recurring data-science work for enterprise teams. You give it data and a business objective e.g "revenue is falling, find out why" and it profiles the data, plans the analysis, writes and runs code in a sandbox, trains and validates models on held-out data, and delivers the model, the report, and a dashboard. Every step is inspectable. It handles the work analytics and operations teams actually have in their backlog: forecasting, churn, anomaly detection, quality analysis, data cleaning, and reporting. Our first customers are teams with valuable data and not enough experienced data scientists to get through that backlog.

Idea

Why did you pick this idea to work on? Do you have domain expertise in this area? How do you know people need what you’re making?
I have spent years doing the job OctOpus automates — ML research, then five years building production data systems at Maersk. Since March 2026 I have also sat on the other side of the table: as Maersk's Innovation Manager I scout and run PoCs with external startups, so I know exactly what an enterprise needs to see before it trusts a product with its data. The gap: AutoML trains a model and stops. Coding agents help an expert write code, but the expert still has to direct the work and judge the result. The hard part of data science is neither — it is deciding what to test, noticing when a result should not be trusted, changing approach, and delivering an answer the business can use. OctOpus does that loop end to end. The intelligence is the loop, not the model. I know people need it because customers keep bringing the same backlog — forecasting, quality analysis, reporting — and they want it finished, not demonstrated. We reached 1,000+ registered users and +25 enterprise engagements in three months with no outbound sales.
Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they don’t?
DataRobot, Dataiku, H2O.ai, cloud AutoML, BI tools, and coding agents like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor. AutoML automates model selection inside a fixed workflow. BI explains the past but builds nothing predictive. Coding agents make experts faster but still require the expert. Companies do not want another ML tool. They want the work done, with clear evidence when the answer should not be trusted. OctOpus owns the full loop — framing, experimentation, validation, delivery — instead of one step of it.
How do or will you make money? How much could you make?
Individuals pay $20/month. Teams pay $500–$2,000/month based on usage and compute. Enterprise contracts start around $35,000/year and include defined workflows, integrations, security, deployment, compute, and support. Our initial market is enterprise operations and analytics teams with recurring forecasting, ETL, classification, optimization, and reporting work. At a $35,000 average contract, 3,000 customers is $105M ARR. The near-term job is proving the pilot-to-contract conversion that makes that arithmetic real.
